THE STORY OF CHELSEA FOOTBALL CLUB

MORE THAN A CENTURY OF PASSION, AMBITION AND TRADITION.



A CLUB BUILT IN BLUE

Chelsea Football Club is a professional football club based in London, England. Founded on 10 March 1905, the club was created with Stamford Bridge as its home and has remained closely connected to the famous stadium throughout its history.

Known as the Blues, Chelsea has developed a strong identity around its famous royal blue colours, Stamford Bridge and its tradition of competing at the highest level of football.

Chelsea is about more than football and trophies. It is about the connection between the club, its players and its supporters. From match days at Stamford Bridge to fans following the team around the world, the Chelsea identity continues to grow.



OUR HISTORY

The Beginning (1905)

Chelsea Football Club was founded on 10 March 1905 at the Rising Sun pub, now known as The Butcher's Hook, Stamford Bridge. The club was created with the purpose of playing at the newly developed Stamford Bridge ground.

The Early Years

During its early years, Chelsea established itself as an important football club in London. Stamford Bridge became a regular meeting place for supporters and the club continued to develop its presence in English football.

Growth And Change

As the decades passed, Chelsea went through several periods of change. Different managers, players and generations of supporters contributed to the development of the club.

The Modern Era

Chelsea's modern history has been marked by major domestic and international success. The club has competed against some of the strongest teams in Europe and has won important honours.

Continuing The Legacy

Chelsea's history continues to develop with every new generation of players, coaches and supporters. The club carries the achievements and traditions of its past while continuing to look towards the future.

MOMENTS IN TIME

1955

First League Title

Chelsea won its first English top-flight league championship in 1955 under manager Ted Drake. The achievement marked an important moment in the club's early history and established Chelsea among England's leading football sides.

1970

FA Cup Glory

Chelsea lifted the FA Cup in 1970 after defeating Leeds United in a famous final replay at Old Trafford. The victory was one of the club's most memorable achievements of the era and strengthened its reputation on the domestic stage.

1998

European Success

Chelsea enjoyed major European success in 1998, winning the UEFA Cup Winners' Cup after defeating VfB Stuttgart. The club also went on to win the UEFA Super Cup that year, establishing Chelsea as a growing force in European football.

2012

Champions Of Europe

Chelsea won its first UEFA Champions League title in 2012 after defeating Bayern Munich in the final in Munich. The dramatic victory represented one of the greatest achievements in the club's history and secured Chelsea's place among Europe's elite.

2021

European Champions Again

Chelsea won the UEFA Champions League for a second time in 2021 after defeating Manchester City in Porto. Kai Havertz scored the decisive goal as Chelsea produced a disciplined performance to lift the trophy and celebrate another historic European triumph.

2025

Another European Chapter

In 2025, Chelsea added another major international achievement to its history by winning the FIFA Club World Cup. The success gave the club another opportunity to compete on the global stage and created a new memorable chapter for Chelsea supporters.

STAMFORD BRIDGE

Stamford Bridge is the historic home of Chelsea Football Club and one of the most recognisable football grounds in England.

Located in west London, the stadium has been closely connected with Chelsea since the club was founded in 1905. For generations of supporters, Stamford Bridge has been the place where some of the club's most important moments have taken place.

Today, Stamford Bridge remains much more than a football stadium. It represents a sense of belonging for Chelsea supporters and continues to bring together players, staff and fans on match days.

OUR VISION

Chelsea Football Club's vision is to continue growing as a successful and respected football club while maintaining the identity and traditions that have shaped its history. The club aims to compete at the highest level, develop talented players and create a future that supporters can be proud of.

Chelsea also looks towards a future where football remains connected to its supporters and communities. Through football, development and ambition, the club continues to inspire people in London and around the world.

OUR MISSION

Chelsea Football Club's mission is to compete with ambition, develop football talent and strive for success across every competition. The club seeks to create an environment where players and staff can develop, perform at their best and contribute to the continued growth of Chelsea.

The club's mission also extends beyond what happens on the football pitch. Chelsea aims to create memorable experiences for its supporters, respect its traditions and strengthen the connection between the club and the people who support it.
