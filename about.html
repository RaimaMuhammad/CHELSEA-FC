A Club Built In Blue

Chelsea Football Club is a professional football club based in London, England. Founded on 10 March 1905, the club was created with Stamford Bridge as its home and has remained closely connected to the famous stadium throughout its history.

Known as the Blues, Chelsea has developed a strong identity around its famous royal blue colours, Stamford Bridge and its tradition of competing at the highest level of football.

Chelsea is about more than football and trophies. It is about the connection between the club, its players and its supporters. From match days at Stamford Bridge to fans following the team around the world, the Chelsea identity continues to grow.

Our History

Chelsea Football Club has a history that stretches back more than a century. Since its formation in 1905, the club has experienced moments of great success, periods of change and challenges that have helped shape its identity.

1905

The Beginning

Chelsea Football Club was founded on 10 March 1905 at the Rising Sun pub, now known as The Butcher's Hook, opposite Stamford Bridge. The club was created with the purpose of playing at the newly developed Stamford Bridge ground.

EARLY YEARS

The Early Years

During its early years, Chelsea established itself as an important football club in London. Stamford Bridge became a regular meeting place for supporters and the club continued to develop its presence in English football.

GROWTH

Growth And Change

As the decades passed, Chelsea went through several periods of change. Different managers, players and generations of supporters contributed to the development of the club.

MODERN ERA

The Modern Era

Chelsea's modern history has been marked by major domestic and international success. The club has competed against some of the strongest teams in Europe and has won important honours.

TODAY

Continuing The Legacy

Chelsea's history continues to develop with every new generation of players, coaches and supporters. The club carries the achievements and traditions of its past while continuing to look towards the future.

THE JOURNEY

Moments In Time

Explore some of the defining moments that have shaped Chelsea Football Club throughout its history.

1905

The Beginning

Chelsea Football Club was founded on 10 March 1905 in London. Stamford Bridge became the club's home, beginning a football story that would span more than a century.

1955

First League Title

Chelsea won its first English top-flight league championship in 1955, marking an important moment in the club's early history.

1970

FA Cup Glory

Chelsea won the FA Cup for the first time in 1970, defeating Leeds United in a memorable final replay.

1998

European Success

Chelsea enjoyed major European success in 1998, winning the UEFA Cup Winners' Cup and the UEFA Super Cup.

2012

Champions Of Europe

Chelsea won its first UEFA Champions League title in 2012, defeating Bayern Munich in the final.

2021

European Champions Again

Chelsea lifted the UEFA Champions League trophy for a second time in 2021 after defeating Manchester City in the final.

2025

Another European Chapter

Chelsea added another European honour to its history in 2025, continuing the club's tradition of competing for major trophies.

OUR HOME

Stamford Bridge

Stamford Bridge is the historic home of Chelsea Football Club and one of the most recognisable football grounds in England.

Located in west London, the stadium has been closely connected with Chelsea since the club was founded in 1905. For generations of supporters, Stamford Bridge has been the place where some of the club's most important moments have taken place.

Today, Stamford Bridge remains much more than a football stadium. It represents a sense of belonging for Chelsea supporters and continues to bring together players, staff and fans on match days.

The Colour Of Chelsea

The colour blue has been at the heart of Chelsea Football Club's identity for generations. It is one of the first things associated with the club and has become a symbol recognised by Chelsea supporters around the world.

Chelsea's colours have developed throughout the club's history. The club originally played in Eton blue before adopting the royal blue that became strongly associated with Chelsea.

The Trophy Cabinet

Chelsea Football Club has built a proud history of success across domestic, European and international competitions. The club's trophy cabinet reflects more than a century of football and includes some of the most important honours in the game.

From the club's first league championship in 1955 to its European successes and international triumphs, Chelsea has continued to establish itself as one of football's most successful clubs.

Chelsea Today

Chelsea Football Club continues to be one of the most recognised football clubs in England and around the world. Today, the club continues to compete at the highest level while working to build a strong and successful future.

A major part of Chelsea's future is the development of talented players. The club continues to give attention to both experienced footballers and young talent, creating opportunities for players to develop their abilities.

Chelsea's identity also continues to be shaped by its supporters. From match days at Stamford Bridge to fans following the club from different parts of the world, the connection between Chelsea and its supporters remains strong.

As Chelsea looks towards the future, the club continues to build on more than a century of history. Today's players, coaches, staff and supporters contribute to the next chapter of the Chelsea story.

Our Vision

Chelsea Football Club's vision is to continue growing as a successful and respected football club while maintaining the identity and traditions that have shaped its history. The club aims to compete at the highest level, develop talented players and create a future that supporters can be proud of.

Chelsea also looks towards a future where football remains connected to its supporters and communities. Through football, development and ambition, the club continues to inspire people in London and around the world.

Our Mission

Chelsea Football Club's mission is to compete with ambition, develop football talent and strive for success across every competition. The club seeks to create an environment where players and staff can develop, perform at their best and contribute to the continued growth of Chelsea.

The club's mission also extends beyond what happens on the football pitch. Chelsea aims to create memorable experiences for its supporters, respect its traditions and strengthen the connection between the club and the people who support it.
