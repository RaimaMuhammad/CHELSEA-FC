CHELSEA FOOTBALL CLUB

THE STORY OF CHELSEA

Discover the history, identity and journey of Chelsea Football Club.

About Chelsea FC

Chelsea Football Club is a professional football club based in London, England. The club was founded on 10 March 1905 and has called Stamford Bridge its home since its formation.

Known as the Blues, Chelsea has developed a strong footballing identity built around ambition, tradition, competition and the support of fans around the world.

Our History

Chelsea's story has developed through more than a century of football, change and achievement.

1905 — The Beginning

Chelsea Football Club was founded on 10 March 1905. The club was created to play at the newly developed Stamford Bridge stadium.

The Early Years

Chelsea quickly became an important part of football in London and attracted large crowds to Stamford Bridge. The club continued to establish itself in English football.

A Club of Change

Throughout the decades, Chelsea experienced periods of success, difficult moments and important changes both on and off the pitch.

The Modern Era

Chelsea has become a major force in English and European football, collecting important domestic and international honours while developing generations of players.

Stamford Bridge

Stamford Bridge is more than Chelsea's home stadium. It is one of the most important parts of the club's identity and history.

The stadium has been Chelsea's home since the club was formed in 1905 and has witnessed generations of players, supporters, important matches and memorable moments.

From its early days as a sporting ground to the modern stadium, Stamford Bridge has continually evolved while remaining closely connected to Chelsea Football Club.

What Makes Chelsea?

The Blues

Chelsea is widely known as the Blues, a nickname closely associated with the club's famous blue colours.

Royal Blue

Blue has been central to Chelsea's visual identity for generations. The club's famous royal blue colours have become one of its most recognisable features.

The Chelsea Badge

The club badge has changed throughout Chelsea's history, but the lion has remained an important symbol of the club's identity.

Major Honours

Chelsea's history has been shaped by success in domestic, European and international competitions.

  • Premier League
  • FA Cup
  • League Cup
  • UEFA Champions League
  • UEFA Europa League
  • UEFA Conference League
  • FIFA Club World Cup

Chelsea Today

Chelsea continues to compete at the highest levels of football while developing talent and building its future.

The Chelsea story is not only about trophies. It is also about the players, coaches, staff and supporters who have contributed to the club across generations.

From Stamford Bridge to supporters around the world, Chelsea remains a club with a strong identity and a continuing ambition for success.
