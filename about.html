About Chelsea FC

Chelsea Football Club is a professional football club based in London, England. Founded on 10 March 1905, the club was created with Stamford Bridge as its home and has remained closely connected to the famous stadium throughout its history. From its beginnings in west London, Chelsea has grown into a club recognised and supported by millions of football fans around the world.

Known as the Blues, Chelsea has built a strong identity around its famous royal blue colours, its historic home at Stamford Bridge and its tradition of competing at the highest level. Over the years, the club has experienced memorable victories, difficult periods, legendary players and important moments that have helped shape the Chelsea we know today. Its history is filled with ambition, determination and a desire to succeed.

Chelsea is about more than football and trophies. It is about the connection between the club, its players and its supporters. From match days at Stamford Bridge to fans following the team from different parts of the world, the Chelsea identity continues to grow. With a proud history behind it and new generations of players and supporters ahead, Chelsea continues to build on its legacy while looking towards the future.

Our History

Chelsea Football Club has a history that stretches back more than a century. Since its formation in 1905, the club has experienced moments of great success, periods of change and challenges that have helped shape its identity. From its early days in London to becoming a recognised name in English and European football, Chelsea's journey has been defined by ambition, determination and the support of its fans.

The Beginning

Chelsea Football Club was founded on 10 March 1905 at the Rising Sun pub, now known as The Butcher's Hook, opposite Stamford Bridge. Unlike many football clubs, Chelsea was created before it had a team, with the purpose of playing at the newly developed Stamford Bridge ground. The club quickly became part of the football culture in London and began building a following among local supporters.

The Early Years

During its early years, Chelsea established itself as an important football club in London. Stamford Bridge became a regular meeting place for supporters and the club continued to develop its presence in English football. Chelsea experienced both successes and setbacks, but its growing support helped establish the foundations for the club's future.

Growth and Change

As the decades passed, Chelsea went through several periods of change. Different managers, players and generations of supporters contributed to the development of the club. Chelsea experienced important achievements as well as difficult seasons, with each period becoming part of the wider history of the Blues.

The Modern Era

Chelsea's modern history has been marked by major domestic and international success. The club has competed against some of the strongest teams in Europe and has won important honours, including the Premier League and UEFA Champions League. Along the way, Chelsea has been represented by talented players and managers who have created memorable moments for supporters around the world.

Continuing the Legacy

Chelsea's history continues to develop with every new generation of players, coaches and supporters. The club carries the achievements and traditions of its past while continuing to look towards the future. From its home at Stamford Bridge to supporters across the world, the journey of Chelsea remains an important part of the club's identity.

Stamford Bridge

Stamford Bridge is the historic home of Chelsea Football Club and one of the most recognisable football grounds in England. Located in Fulham, west London, the stadium has been closely connected with Chelsea since the club was founded in 1905. For generations of supporters, Stamford Bridge has been the place where some of the club's most important moments have taken place.

The stadium has witnessed countless matches, celebrations, important victories and memorable performances from players who have worn the Chelsea shirt. It has also developed alongside the club, changing over the years to meet the needs of modern football while maintaining its connection with Chelsea's history and supporters.

Today, Stamford Bridge remains much more than a football stadium. It represents a sense of belonging for Chelsea supporters and continues to bring together players, staff and fans on match days. Its history and atmosphere make it an important part of what Chelsea Football Club represents.

Club Colours

The colour blue has been at the heart of Chelsea Football Club's identity for generations. It is one of the first things associated with the club and has become a symbol recognised by Chelsea supporters around the world.

Chelsea's colours have developed throughout the club's history. The club originally played in Eton blue before adopting the royal blue that became strongly associated with Chelsea. Over the years, blue has remained a defining feature of the team's football shirts and other club merchandise.

Today, Chelsea's blue colours represent more than the team's kit. They are part of the club's identity and connect generations of players and supporters. Whether at Stamford Bridge or watching from different parts of the world, Chelsea fans proudly wear the blue of the Blues.

The Trophy Cabinet

Chelsea Football Club has built a proud history of success across domestic, European and international competitions. The club's trophy cabinet reflects more than a century of football and includes some of the most important honours in the game.

From the club's first league championship in 1955 to its European successes and international triumphs, Chelsea has continued to establish itself as one of football's most successful clubs.

First Division 1955 Chelsea won its first English top-flight league title in 1955.
Premier League 2005, 2006, 2010, 2015, 2017 Chelsea has won the Premier League five times.

League Cup 1965, 1998, 2005, 2007, 2015 Chelsea has won the League Cup five times.

Chelsea Today

Chelsea Football Club continues to be one of the most recognised football clubs in England and around the world. Today, the club continues to compete at the highest level while working to build a strong and successful future.

A major part of Chelsea's future is the development of talented players. The club continues to give attention to both experienced footballers and young talent, creating opportunities for players to develop their abilities.

Chelsea's identity also continues to be shaped by its supporters. From match days at Stamford Bridge to fans following the club from different parts of the world, the connection between Chelsea and its supporters remains strong.

As Chelsea looks towards the future, the club continues to build on more than a century of history. Today's players, coaches, staff and supporters contribute to the next chapter of the Chelsea story.

Our Vision

Chelsea Football Club's vision is to continue growing as a successful and respected football club while maintaining the identity and traditions that have shaped its history. The club aims to compete at the highest level, develop talented players and create a future that supporters can be proud of.

Chelsea also looks towards a future where football remains connected to its supporters and communities. Through football, development and ambition, the club continues to inspire people in London and around the world.

Our Mission

Chelsea Football Club's mission is to compete with ambition, develop football talent and strive for success across every competition. The club seeks to create an environment where players and staff can develop, perform at their best and contribute to the continued growth of Chelsea.

The club's mission also extends beyond what happens on the football pitch. Chelsea aims to create memorable experiences for its supporters, respect its traditions and strengthen the connection between the club and the people who support it.
